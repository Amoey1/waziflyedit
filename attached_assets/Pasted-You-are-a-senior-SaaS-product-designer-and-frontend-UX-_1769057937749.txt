You are a senior SaaS product designer and frontend UX architect.

I want to visually align my existing SaaS website (Wazifly) with the
design language and themes commonly used by Salla and Zid platforms,
WITHOUT copying their UI or violating brand guidelines.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT rebuild the website
- Do NOT change routes or core layout
- Only refine theme, spacing, colors, and visual hierarchy

--------------------------------
GOAL
--------------------------------
Make it immediately clear that:
- Wazifly is built specifically for Salla & Zid merchants
- The platform feels familiar and trustworthy to existing Salla/Zid users
- The design feels like a natural extension of their ecosystem

--------------------------------
DESIGN ALIGNMENT GUIDELINES
--------------------------------

1) VISUAL STYLE (SIMILAR, NOT IDENTICAL)

Align with Salla & Zid by adopting:
- Clean, spacious layouts
- Soft backgrounds with subtle color blocks
- Rounded cards and containers
- Calm, professional SaaS color palettes
- Clear typography hierarchy (Headlines / Body / UI labels)

Avoid:
- Copying exact colors
- Replicating UI components
- Mimicking dashboards or layouts pixel-by-pixel

--------------------------------
2) COLOR & THEME DIRECTION

- Base theme should remain Wazifly green (Saudi / enterprise identity)
- Introduce subtle accent hints inspired by:
  - Salla: soft mint / teal tones
  - Zid: light purple / lavender tones

Usage:
- Accents only (icons, dividers, highlights)
- Never overpower the Wazifly brand

--------------------------------
3) LOGO & PLATFORM AFFINITY (VERY IMPORTANT)

Add official Salla and Zid logos to the Wazifly website in a
"platform compatibility" context.

Acceptable placements:
- A small “Built for” or “Works with” section near the hero
- A subtle integration strip below the main hero
- Inside a trust / compatibility section

Rules:
- Logos must be secondary to Wazifly
- Use monochrome or softened versions
- Do NOT place them in the header as primary branding
- Do NOT imply ownership or partnership

Text examples:
- “Built specifically for Salla & Zid merchants”
- “Designed to work natively with Salla and Zid stores”

--------------------------------
4) UI COMPONENT REFINEMENT

Refine components to feel platform-native:
- Cards should feel dashboard-like but lightweight
- Buttons should be confident and calm (not aggressive)
- Icons should be simple, line-based, enterprise-style

--------------------------------
5) PAGE COVERAGE

Apply the visual alignment consistently across:
- Home
- AI Employees
- How It Works
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Dashboard Preview

--------------------------------
FINAL CHECK
--------------------------------
The final result should make a Salla or Zid merchant think:
“This looks familiar. This feels built for my store.”

Do NOT mention competitors negatively.
Do NOT use marketing hype.
Focus on clarity, trust, and ecosystem fit.
