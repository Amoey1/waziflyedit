You are a senior SaaS product marketer and UX copywriter specialized in AI-powered eCommerce platforms.

Design a complete multi-page website for a new SaaS product that serves eCommerce merchants on Salla and Zid (Saudi Arabia) by providing Action-Taking AI Agents that work like real employees.

The AI agents are able to analyze situations, propose decisions, request merchant approval, and then execute real actions inside the store and communication channels.

Product Information

Product name: Opero AI

Product type: SaaS (subscription-based)

Market: Saudi Arabia (scalable to GCC)

Target users: Salla & Zid merchants (non-technical, business-focused)

Language: English

Tone: Professional, clear, confident, human, non-technical

Core Value Proposition

Opero AI provides AI Employees that manage daily store operations such as sales, customer support, marketing, operations, and analytics.

These AI employees do not act blindly.
Every sensitive action follows this model:
Decision → Approval → Execution → Log

Merchants stay fully in control while saving time and increasing revenue.
